BreFlo

Calm offline breathing companion. Rhythm, length, visual. No account, no ads, no analytics.

Privacy Policy

Effective 21 August 2026 · App package com.laartheuser.breflo

Summary

BreFlo does not create an account, does not use the internet, and does not send analytics. Your techniques and settings stay on your device.

What BreFlo stores

On this device only (Android DataStore), BreFlo may store:

  • Breathing techniques and session length preferences
  • Motion, color, overlay, sound, and design choices
  • Language preference when you override the system language

There is no BreFlo cloud and no BreFlo server that receives this data.

Permissions

BreFlo may use the VIBRATE permission for an optional soft pulse when a breath phase changes. You can turn vibration off in Sound settings.

BreFlo does not request internet, location, contacts, microphone, camera, or notification access for its core experience.

Audio

Optional phase tones and ambient loops play from files bundled with the app. They are not streamed from a network. Ambient sound uses the media volume path; phase tones follow the device sonification / silent-mode policy.

Android backup

If Android backup / restore is enabled on your phone, your BreFlo settings may be copied to the same Google account or restored after a device transfer. That is an Android system feature, not a BreFlo server.

Children

BreFlo does not target children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone. There is no account and no remote profile.

Wellness note

BreFlo is a breathing companion for everyday calm. It is not medical advice and is not a medical device. It is not a substitute for professional care. If you have a health condition, ask a clinician before changing how you breathe.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated on this page. Material changes will be reflected in the in-app Info screen where practical.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the BreFlo GitHub repository or contact the publisher listed on the Google Play store listing for BreFlo.
